# Paintbrush
![icon.png](src/main/resources/assets/paintbrush/icon.png) 
> This mod makes life easier for builders by breathing new life into the brush with painting capabilities!

**The mod introduces an item that inherits the behavior of the brush while featuring unique textures and mechanics.**

**Paintbrush:**

* The entire mod revolves around it 🥶🤓
* **Crafting:** Hold a brush in one hand and the desired dye in the other, then hold RMB on any block. The brush turns into a Paintbrush of the corresponding color, consuming 1 dye and 1 durability.
* **Appearance:** Resembles a standard brush dipped in colored paint.
* **Painting Blocks:** Hold the paintbrush in one hand and the matching dye in the other, then hold RMB on a target block of a different color. It consumes 1 dye and 1 durability to dye the block (only works on blocks from the Colored Blocks tab).
* **Tinting Glass:** Hold a black paintbrush in one hand and an amethyst shard in the other, then hold RMB on glass of any color. Consumes 1 amethyst shard and 1 durability to turn it into tinted glass.
* **Cleaning via Water Bottle:** Hold the paintbrush in one hand and a water bottle in the other, then hold RMB on any block. It turns back into a regular brush, consuming 1 durability and returning an empty glass bottle.
* **Cleaning via Cauldron:** Right-click a water cauldron with the paintbrush to turn it back into a regular brush, depleting 1 level of water.
* **Stripping Paint from Blocks:** Hold a brush in one hand and a water bottle in the other, then right-click a colored block (terracotta, glass, glass pane, tinted glass, shulker box, or candle). The block reverts to its uncolored state, consuming 1 durability from the brush and returning an empty bottle.
* **Particles:** Dynamically spawns block dust particles during use depending on whether the target block can be painted.
* **Durability:** Has 64 durability points.
* **Enchantments:** Can be enchanted with Unbreaking, Mending, Efficiency, and Curse of Vanishing.
* **Base Usage Speed:** Default usage time is 40 ticks.
* **Status Effects:** Affected by Haste and Mining Fatigue.
* **Repairing:** Can be repaired on an anvil using String.
* **Combining:** Can be combined with another paintbrush (vanilla mechanic).
* **Default Color:** White.

The mod runs on Fabric starting from Java 26.2 and requires Fabric API.
